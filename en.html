Why use a cannabis tracker

Keeping a log shows you your real habits, not the ones you think you have: how many sessions a day, at what times, and how much you spend each month. JointTracker is built to be honest and judgment-free, whether you just want to keep an eye on things or you're planning a tolerance break or cutting down.

It's a Progressive Web App (PWA): it goes on your home screen like a native app, but there's no app store and no manual updates, and it works offline too.
