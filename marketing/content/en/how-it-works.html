How JointTracker works

JointTracker is designed to log a session in a few seconds and turn that data into stats that are useful over time. Here's how the main features work.

1. Log a session

From the "Add" page you choose what you used (hash, weed or both), the amount in grams, and the date and time. You can add a context (relax, social, creative, sleep), a mood rating after the session and, if you want, a photo and GPS location.

2. Track streaks and tolerance breaks

The home screen shows your streak of consecutive days with logged sessions. On the Stats page you can set and follow a tolerance break: a planned period off, with a history of past breaks and their effect on your tolerance.

3. Read stats and charts

The Stats page works out daily, monthly and yearly averages, compares the current month with the previous one and shows automatic milestones (achievements). The Charts page adds a calendar heatmap, trends over time, the breakdown by time of day and day of the week, and monthly spending based on the purchases you've actually used.

4. Manage stash and spending

On the Stash page you log your purchases (grams and price), and the app automatically draws the amounts down from your active stash as you log sessions.

5. Places map

You can save the places you go (from GPS or by searching the map) and see usage stats for each one.

6. Works offline and can be installed

JointTracker is a Progressive Web App: you can install it to your phone's home screen like a normal app. Data you've already loaded stays available with no connection, and syncs as soon as you're back online.

How to install it: on Android/Chrome you'll find the "📲 Install the app" button right on the home page (it only appears if the browser supports automatic installation); otherwise you can always find it inside the app, under Settings. On iPhone/Safari there is no install button: tap the Share icon and choose "Add to Home Screen". Either way, avoid using your browser menu's "Add to Home Screen" directly on this page or the home page: on Android that only creates a link to the site instead of the real app.

7. Guest mode or account

You can try the app straight away without signing up: your data stays only on your device. If you later want to keep it safe, sync it across several devices or use social features (friends, leaderboards, push notifications), you can create an account at any time without losing the data you entered in guest mode.
