Why use a cannabis tracker

Keeping a usage diary helps you see your real habits rather than the ones you assume: how many sessions a day, at what times, and how much you spend each month. JointTracker is built to be an honest, non-judgemental tool, made both for people who just want to keep track and for those planning a tolerance break or looking to cut down on cannabis.

It's a Progressive Web App (PWA): it installs to your home screen like a native app, but needs no app store and no manual updates, and it works offline too.
