Frequently asked questions

How does the tolerance break tracker work?

From the Stats page you can set the start of a break (tolerance break) and the app keeps track of the days that have passed, the history of previous breaks and how long they lasted, so you can work out how much and when to take a break based on your real habits.

Is my data private?

Yes. In guest mode your data stays only on your device and is never sent anywhere. With an account, your data is stored in a protected database (Supabase) that only your user can access; social features show other people only what you choose to share (e.g. accepted friendships).

Is JointTracker free?

Yes, JointTracker is completely free, with no ads and no subscriptions.

Does it work offline?

Yes. It's a Progressive Web App (PWA): after the first load you can keep using it with no connection, and your changes sync automatically as soon as you're back online.

Do I need to sign up to use it?

No, you can try it straight away in guest mode without creating an account. Some features (photos, push notifications, automatic backups, social features) require signing up, but you can convert your guest account into a real one at any time without losing your data.

How do I install JointTracker on my phone?

On Android/Chrome you'll find the "Install the app" button right on the home page, or inside the app under Settings. On iPhone/Safari there is no button: tap the Share icon and choose "Add to Home Screen". Once installed it behaves like a normal app, with an icon on your home screen.

Does JointTracker track spending too?

Yes, on the Stash page you log your purchases (grams and price) and on the Charts page you'll find monthly spending, worked out by spreading the cost of each purchase across the months in which it was actually used.

Can I use it with friends?

Yes, with an account you can add friends, log shared sessions and see leaderboards (global, among friends, or just for the sessions you did together).
